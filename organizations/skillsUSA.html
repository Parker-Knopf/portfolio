{% include heading3-section.html title="About" subtitle="General Info" %}

SkillsUSA is a United States career and technical student organization serving more than 395,000 high school, college and middle school students and professional members enrolled in training programs in trade, technical and skilled service occupations, including health occupations. This organization impacts students, advancing their future beyond academic classes. The values of this organization promotes students' ability to apply technical education and leadership to the workforce after high school or college.

{% include heading3-section.html title="Leadership" subtitle="President" %}

With the support of teachers and administration, I built this program from the ground up. I was delighted to share my knowledge, experience, and expertise with peers. I utilized my expertise to assist and prepare students for careers beyond high school. My role as elected president gave me the opportunity to contribute back to the CTE community.

{% include heading3-section.html title="Competition" subtitle="3D Printing" %}

Additive Manufacturing was the first competition I competed in as a SkillsUSA member. The regional “competition” featured a job interview regarding additive manufacturing. The judge was so impressed with my presentation that he personally took me aside expressing his outlook on my bright future ahead. I'll never forget what that judge told me, laying the foundations to my confidence as a public speaker forever.

{% include heading3-section.html title="Competition" subtitle="AMT" %}

This competition, Automated Manufacturing Technology (AMT), is a CAD, CAM, and CNC milling competition. It calls for a three man team and involves everything from design to a finished product. I was in charge of CAM, and also acted as the middleman/team leader. The competition took around 6 hours and time was always a concern.

{% include image-element.html image="/assets/images/organizations/skillsUSA/nationalPart2-crop.jpg" alt="Competition" %}
{% include heading3-section.html title="Automated Manufacturing Technology" subtitle="NATIONAL FIRST PLACE MEDALIST" %}

Shown are the components manufactured during the national first place competition. This competition tested for speed, precision, and professionalism. In 6 hours we had designed, coded, and manufactured all components in record time, awarding us the Gold Metal!
