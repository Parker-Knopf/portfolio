{% include heading3-section.html title="Plate" subtitle="CNC Manufacture" %}

One of the biggest tasks to be done for this project was the system structure, supporting all sensors and actuators. These plates acted as the backbone to the entire system. There exists 4 plates to the testing bed, all self-designed though collaborative meetings. These plates once designed were personally CNC manufactured using a HAAS machine. The CAM programing was preformed in Fusion.

{% include image-element.html image="/assets/images/teamProjects/oceanWell/ROVThruster.png" alt="GUI Interface" %}
{% include heading3-section.html title="Thruster" subtitle="Sub-assembly" %}

This assembly was one of the most curtail components to the system. The assembly was self-designed to fit the off-the-shelf Blue Robotics thruster. The intent of this assembly was to act as an axial pump, per request of the sponsor, to suck water through the reverse osmosis membrane. The assembly was designed as a two part funnel to maximize thruster efficiency in transporting fluid.
