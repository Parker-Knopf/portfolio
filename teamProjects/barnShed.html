This project was a family project my father, brother and I conducted. With my engineering expertise in CAD and understanding of California law and code for building construction I designed the barn and we built it.

{% include image-element.html image="/assets/images/teamProjects/barnShed/drawing3.png" alt="CAD" %}
{% include heading3-section.html title="Drawings" subtitle="AutoCad Drafting" %}

I have no expertise in architecture. My knowledge in engineering and skills in AutoCAD paved the way to drawings utilized to design and construct the barn shed. The design of the structure is entirely from scratch with no reference model, and only a vision between my father and I.

{% include image-element.html image="/assets/images/teamProjects/barnShed/drawing2.png" alt="CAD" %}
{% include heading3-section.html title="Construction" subtitle="Barn Frame" %}

The barn was constructed as a team with my Dad, my brother, and myself. I directed the project utilizing drawings I had created and used on site as blueprints. Constructing this barn illustrates my ability to simultaneously design and apply the hands-on work required for the engineering field.
