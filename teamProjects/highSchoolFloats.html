Every year for homecoming, the school would host half-time class competitions with floats. With the students constructing the floats and with my reputation I naturally became the lead engineer in this project.

{% include image-element.html image="/assets/images/teamProjects/highSchoolFloats/frame.png" alt="CAD" %}
{% include heading3-section.html title="Legacy" subtitle="Standardizing" %}

The school had been holding this competition for years before I was even a student at the school. Throughout all that time however, no float was ever worth keeping due to its poor design. The float base I designed and constructed however was lightweight, cheap, and big meeting all the criteria. This design proved to be so successful the class advisor asked for a copy of the design to set as a standard for all future float bases. With this design I had in yet another way left a piece of me as a legacy at the high school.
