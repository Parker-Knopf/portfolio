A high school physics class team project. Construction materials were limited to ½” shafts of wood, paper, glue, and a waterproof sealant of our choice. The objective was to speedrun a lap in the pool.

{% include image-element.html image="/assets/images/teamProjects/physicsBoat/cad.png" alt="CAD" %}
{% include heading3-section.html title="Design" subtitle="CAD Development" %}

My team had decided to brainstorm ideas for the project independently and choose a design we saw best fit. The result was my CAD model in comparison to hand sketches. This design was originally intended only as a concept, however became the blueprints.
