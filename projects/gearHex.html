{% include heading3-section.html title="About" subtitle="General Info" %}

This fully 3D printed assembly converts 400 rotations of motion into 1 singular rotation through an unconventional method. Using two harmonic drives offset by one tooth, creates this drastic gear ratio.

{% include heading3-section.html title="Design" subtitle="Harmonic Drive" %}

This project was entirely inspired and reversed engineered from a similar project found on the web. The mechanism uses two harmonic drives each with a gear ratio of 20:1 to create a 400:1 gear reduction. All components of this design including the flexible material were fabricated with additive manufacturing methods. The design of the assembly was carefully engineered to yield a simplistic and intact product.

{% include heading3-section.html title="Research" subtitle="Gear Design" %}

Developing this mechanism came with research on spur gears and harmonic drives. Devoting numerous hours to the study of gear design allowed gear teeth to be incorporated into my mechanism.
